# Personal portfolio v3.6.0 — Connect Suite and contextual Quick Hub

Preview branch `sudipdasin/sudip-das-frontend@preview-personalweb` only. Production, Worker API, protected owner records, and historical WebStudio theme are unchanged.

## Let’s Connect panel
The real contact section is enhanced into three grounded groups: Direct (email, copy action, topic-specific mailto links), Professional (published LinkedIn and GitHub profiles), and Social & Creative (published YouTube channel). Icons are first-party inline SVG and all links reuse URLs already present in the site. No invented messaging accounts, automatic messages, promises of response, third-party social SDKs, or analytics are added. Clipboard copy falls back to a visible selectable input.

## Quick Hub
Four keyboard-operable tabs: Explore, Themes, Tools, Connect. Tools contains two small expandable, independent preview windows: Request selected profile details (six category names and a clear statement that submission is disabled on public preview) and WebStudio (three illustrated local SVG chapters). Both link to their *already-existing* complete in-page experiences. Connect tab links to the same approved public email/LinkedIn/GitHub/YouTube destinations and the full contact panel. The miniature panels also appear below the full Let's Connect grid.

## Presentation and safety
Theme-adaptive layered acrylic surfaces, graded backgrounds, colored icons, outlines, soft focus and hover accents, and reduced-motion fallback. No change to private request submission or owner permissions. No background CI was triggered: owner instructed avoidance of GitHub/CircleCI credits. Source and syntax checks only; deployed appearance needs owner screenshot/functional feedback.
