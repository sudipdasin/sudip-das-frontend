THE ENDURING WEB PRESENCE PRINCIPLE

Design for recovery. Not dependence.

One broken provider should not erase your identity. Web Studio is being designed to make backups, restoration and provider switching manageable from one place.

1 · DetectCheck site, DNS, TLS and content integrity; distinguish outages from configuration errors and attacks.
2 · PreserveKeep encrypted, versioned, tested copies in local and independent cloud destinations.
3 · RecoverRestore healthy content, redeploy to an alternate host and use verified DNS change procedures.
4 · VerifyCheck HTTPS, content, forms and services after recovery; alert the owner and retain rollback options.

Reliability goal, not an uptime guarantee: domain expiration, DNS caching, registry policies, attacks and provider outages can still interrupt service. Automatic failover and recovery are under development and require real-world validation.
