THE ENDURING WEB PRESENCE PRINCIPLE

Design for recovery. Not dependence.

One broken provider should not erase your identity. Web Studio is being designed to make backups, restoration and provider switching manageable from one place.

1 · DetectCheck site, DNS, TLS and content integrity; distinguish outages from configuration errors and attacks.
2 · PreserveKeep encrypted, versioned, tested copies in local and independent cloud destinations.
3 · RecoverRestore healthy content, redeploy to an alternate host and use verified DNS change procedures.
4 · VerifyCheck HTTPS, content, forms and services after recovery; alert the owner and retain rollback options.

Reliability goal, not an uptime guarantee: domain expiration, DNS caching, registry policies, attacks and provider outages can still interrupt service. Automatic failover and recovery are under development and require real-world validation.

DEVDAS DEVELOPMENT · PRODUCT INFORMATION

About WebStudio

WebStudio is a website and portfolio-building product developed by DevDas Development, currently available as an interactive fictional demo ahead of its public release.

What works in this demo

Six visual layouts with matching avatars, browser-local profile editing, project management, language switching, manual JSON export and import, and a responsive portfolio preview.

What is not live yet

Customer accounts, secure hosting, domain setup, payments, automated backups, and production publishing are planned. This public demo does not accept real private credentials or financial records.

PART OF A BIGGER VISION

WebStudio is part of the LifeSphere suite

LifeSphere is a separate, evolving personal digital-life application from DevDas Development. Its private identity, document, finance and security tools do not run inside WebStudio.

LifeSphere product areas (separate from WebStudio):

  • Private Vault: local encrypted credentials and sensitive records — development prototype, not audited for production.
  • Documents & Renewals: local OCR-assisted document management and date reminders — development prototype.
  • Exposure Watch: local incident tracking prototype; automated breach monitoring is not yet implemented.
  • Identity & Government Services: planned consent-based connectors; no live government integration.
  • Cloud synchronization: optional encrypted backups and cross-device support are planned.

LifeSphere is not available through this WebStudio demo. No private LifeSphere records are transferred to, stored in, or published by WebStudio.
